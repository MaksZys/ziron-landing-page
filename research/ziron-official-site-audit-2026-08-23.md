# Ziron official-site audit — 2026-08-23

Scope: read-only review of the publicly accessible official `ziron.pl` site. This records what the source supports, rather than proposed copy or unverified claims.

## Executive conclusion

Ziron is best understood as a small, two-person visual-content studio that creates marketing assets for machinery-heavy businesses, with the clearest intended niche being agriculture and adjacent technical/industrial sectors. The substantive, visible offer is photography, end-to-end film production, drone filming, and optional social-media management. Both founders participate across filming, photography, aerial capture, editing, and graphic post-production.

The current source has an important positioning conflict: visible content leads with professional video production for companies, while titles, metadata, and the Instagram handle position Ziron as an agro-marketing agency that also runs Meta Ads. A refresh must choose the actual primary promise and then make the hero, services, SEO metadata, portfolio, and social presence consistent. The published body copy supports visual production far more specifically than it supports paid-media management.

## Evidence currently missing

There are no named client projects, case studies, testimonials, results, partner logos, awards, certifications, legal entity details, physical address, NIP/REGON, founding year, employee count, pricing, or careers material on the audited public site. The services page contains five YouTube embeds, but no accompanying project descriptions or outcomes: [services](https://ziron.pl/czym-sie-zajmujemy).

The refresh should source verified material for a work portfolio (client permission, goal, deliverables, and outcome), testimonial attribution, team roles, and legal/trust information. Do not manufacture performance claims from the current copy.

## Issues to resolve in the refresh

1. **Choose one positioning.** The visible hero says “Profesjonalna produkcja wideo dla firm,” whereas page titles and metadata call the business agricultural marketing and mention Meta Ads. The content should either lead with visual production for agro/industry, or substantiate a broader marketing/paid-media offer before leading with it.
2. **Fix the homepage typo.** “bezładna wycena” appears where “bezpłatna wycena” (free estimate) is likely intended: [home](https://ziron.pl/).
3. **Make the offer legible.** The four service headings are present, but no descriptions, deliverables, selection criteria, or proof sit beside them: [services](https://ziron.pl/czym-sie-zajmujemy).
4. **Add a real portfolio and proof.** The existing imagery and YouTube embeds are not captioned as projects. Pair each selected project with client-approved context and results.
5. **Repair lead capture.** The contact form is Polish/English mixed; it is a `GET` form posting back to `/contact`, and its field controls have no names, so the source exposes no functional delivery path. A newsletter-like second form repeats the pattern. The refresh needs one clear, working conversion path with explicit validation and consent: [contact](https://ziron.pl/contact).
6. **Replace the privacy content.** The contact page repeats “Polityka prywatności – ZION” content for a mobile machine/vehicle-washing company. It is unrelated to Ziron and must not be carried forward. Publish ZIRON-specific controller identity, purposes, lawful bases, recipients, retention, rights, cookies, and contact information: [contact](https://ziron.pl/contact).
7. **Remove template routes.** The publicly indexed Classic Cap and Group fitness class pages weaken credibility: [Classic Cap](https://ziron.pl/classic-cap), [Group fitness class](https://ziron.pl/group-fitness-class).
8. **Normalize language and trust signals.** Use intentional Polish or a deliberate multilingual system; do not mix labels such as “Last name,” “Your email,” and “Message” with Polish UI. Update the footer’s 2025 year and confirm the external social destinations.

## Recommended information architecture

1. Homepage: a single chosen niche/promise, selected work, primary CTA.
2. Services: Photography, Film Production, Drone, Social Media—each with what it solves and concrete deliverables only once confirmed.
3. Work: named, permissioned projects with client context, visual assets, and verified outcomes.
4. About: Mateusz Kulesza and Mateusz Radkiewicz, roles, complementary skills, and the small-team ownership advantage.
5. Process: Conversation → tailored concept → production → post-production → optional publication/social media.
6. Contact: one usable form, phone/email alternatives, privacy consent directly beneath the form, and a separate ZIRON privacy page.
